Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 23.11.2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PinMuse ("we", "us", "our") processes personal data when you use the PinMuse mobile app and the website pinmuse.com (together, the "Service"). The controller responsible for data processing is the operator named in our Imprint.

PinMuse is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Pinterest. Pinterest is a trademark of Pinterest, Inc.

1. Summary

  • You sign in with Apple or Pinterest. We do not use passwords.
  • Pinterest access is read-only. We never post, edit or delete anything on Pinterest, and we do not store copies of Pins.
  • AI features (clothing recognition, outfit matching, outfit suggestions) send images or descriptions to OpenAI. Background removal for wardrobe photos runs on your device. We ask for your consent before the first use.
  • Your data is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models.
  • You can delete your account and all data at any time in the app.

2. Data We Process

  • Sign in with Apple: your Apple user ID and the email address Apple shares with us (this may be a private relay address). We also store an Apple token that allows us to revoke the Apple sign-in when you delete your account.
  • Sign in with / connection to Pinterest: your Pinterest user ID and username, used to link your PinMuse account (see section 3).
  • Wardrobe: photos of clothing items you take or upload, the cut-out version without background, and item details (e.g. category, color, material, season) that you enter or that our AI suggests.
  • Saved looks and outfits: the wardrobe items you combined, a match score, a short explanation, missing pieces and, for Pinterest looks, the ID of the inspiration Pin. If weather was used, the weather at that time and the city name are saved with the look.
  • Approximate location: only if you allow it, for weather-based suggestions (see section 5).
  • Usage counts: the number and time of styling suggestions you requested, to apply the free monthly limit.
  • App usage statistics: whether you opened the app on a given day and when a look was generated. No content, no device identifiers, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Subscription status: whether you have PinMuse Premium and until when. Payment details are handled only by Apple or Google; we never see them.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider processes IP addresses and request logs to operate and secure the Service.
  • Support requests: what you send us by email.

3. Pinterest Integration

When you connect Pinterest, PinMuse uses the official Pinterest API with read-only permissions: your account (user_accounts:read), your boards including secret boards (boards:read, boards:read_secret) and the Pins on them (pins:read, pins:read_secret).

  • Access token: stored only on your device in the secure system keychain. During sign-in it passes through our server once and is deleted there immediately after your device has picked it up.
  • Boards and Pins are loaded live from Pinterest whenever you view them. We do not store Pin images, image URLs, titles, descriptions or board data.
  • What we store:
    • your Pinterest user ID and username (account link);
    • the Pin ID of looks you save, so the Pin can be loaded live again;
    • our own AI analysis of a Pin you selected (detected clothing items, style, colors, season), so the same Pin does not have to be analyzed again. It is kept for at most 30 days;
    • the result of a match (your matched items, explanation, missing pieces).
  • Deletion: when you disconnect Pinterest in the app, we delete the Pinterest link, the Pin IDs in your looks and all Pin analyses. If a Pin no longer exists on Pinterest, we remove its ID and analysis as soon as the app notices. Everything is deleted when you delete your account.
  • You can also revoke PinMuse's access in your Pinterest settings at pinterest.com/settings/security.

Your use of Pinterest is subject to Pinterest's Privacy Policy.

4. AI Features

Before you use an AI feature for the first time, the app asks for your consent. Without consent you can still use PinMuse and enter wardrobe items manually.

  • OpenAI (USA) receives
    • wardrobe photos, to recognize the clothing item and suggest details;
    • the image of a Pinterest Pin you select, to recognize the outfit shown;
    • text descriptions of your wardrobe items, the weather and the selected occasion, to find matching items, write explanations, suggest outfits and translate item details.
    OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models and keeps it for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring before deletion.

Removing the background from wardrobe photos happens entirely on your device (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit on Android). Your photos are not sent to any service for this.

AI suggestions are recommendations only and may be inaccurate.

5. Location and Weather

  • Location is used only if you allow it in the system dialog and in the app settings.
  • Your device rounds the coordinates to about 1 km and sends them directly to OpenWeather (OpenWeather Ltd., UK) to get the current weather. We do not store coordinates on our servers.
  • The city name and weather are saved only as part of a look you save.
  • You can turn location off at any time; all other features keep working.

6. Purposes and Legal Bases

  • Providing the Service (account, wardrobe, looks, Pinterest connection, usage limit, subscription): performance of our contract with you, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • AI features and location: your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw consent at any time in the app settings, with effect for the future.
  • App usage statistics, security and abuse prevention: our legitimate interest in a stable, secure Service and in understanding which features are used, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.
  • Legal obligations, e.g. tax records: Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR.

7. Service Providers and International Transfers

  • Supabase — database, file storage and server functions; servers in the EU (Ireland).
  • OpenAI — AI analysis and text generation (USA).
  • OpenWeather — weather data (UK).
  • RevenueCat — subscription management; receives only a pseudonymous user ID and purchase status (USA).
  • Apple / Google — sign-in (Apple), app distribution and payments.
  • Pinterest — source of your boards and Pins (see section 3).

We use these providers only as far as needed for the Service. Where data is transferred to countries outside the EU/EEA, this is based on an adequacy decision (UK; EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework for certified providers) or on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How Long We Keep Data

  • Account, wardrobe, saved looks, usage counts and statistics: until you delete your account.
  • Deleted wardrobe items: recoverable in "Recently deleted" for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Pin analyses: at most 30 days, earlier on Pinterest disconnect or account deletion.
  • Internal records linking the steps of a styling request: 60 days.
  • Pinterest access token on our server: only for the moment of sign-in.
  • Deletion records: to prove deletions to Pinterest, we log the type and time of a deletion without any user ID.

9. Account & Data Deletion

You can delete your PinMuse account and all associated data at any time:

  • In the app: go to Settings → Delete account and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately and permanently, and Sign in with Apple is revoked.
  • Without the app: send an email to moc.esumnip@olleh with the subject "Delete my account". Include the Support ID shown in the app settings if you can. We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

This removes your profile, wardrobe items and photos, saved looks, Pinterest link, Pin analyses, usage counts and statistics. Apple and Google keep their own purchase records under their policies; an active subscription must be cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to your data is restricted to your own account by database access rules; AI and Pinterest requests run through our server functions so that keys and tokens are not exposed.

11. Children

PinMuse is not directed at children under 16, or under the lower minimum age that applies in your country (never under 13). If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15);
  • have inaccurate data corrected (Art. 16);
  • have your data deleted (Art. 17);
  • restrict processing (Art. 18);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21);
  • withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3));
  • lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77).

To exercise your rights, contact us at the address below. We respond within one month.
